Elden Ring — A Text RPG

Rise, Tarnished.

A fan-made, text-based retelling of Elden Ring: create a character, choose your weapons, and battle a gauntlet of bosses in time-based combat. It plays entirely in your browser — nothing to install.

How to play

  • Menus are numbered. Type the number of your choice and press Enter, or select one of the on-screen buttons.
  • Story screens wait for you. Press Enter (or select Continue) to advance.
  • Combat is turn-based: each boss attack charges up while you Attack, Dodge, Heal, or Wait. Dodge in time and in a correct direction to avoid the hit.
  • If you enter something that isn't an option, you simply return to the same prompt — you can't break anything.

Type your choice, then press Enter.
